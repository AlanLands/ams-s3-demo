US-2026-046: Confirmation Pack Wording For Rostered Applicants Admitted As Guests

Requested by: MapleSure Plan Administration
Application: DocumentHub (enrolment document service)
Priority: P3 - enhancement

Business objective:
Give DocumentHub a confirmation pack for the recipient it is about to start
receiving and has no wording for — a person listed on the plan sponsor's own
roster whose enrolment was authorised under Guest access — so that the pack
stops telling a rostered person that MapleSure holds no record of them.

Target member type:
Someone the plan sponsor lists on the contract roster who holds no active
benefit, and whose enrolment EnrolDirect admitted under the Guest access
preference. In the feed this is `onRoster = true` together with an
`authorisingPreference` of "Online Enrolment - Guest".

This combination does not occur in DocumentHub's history. It begins occurring
the day US-2026-045 ships in EnrolDirect, which settles that a prospect is
checked against the Guest preference. Nothing about that change is wrong, and
nothing about it is DocumentHub's to alter — it is an access decision made
upstream. What arrives here is a new kind of recipient.

Description:
DocumentHub holds whole worded packs per audience and selects one, rather than
one letter with substitutions. There are two: the member pack, for someone
already holding coverage under the contract, and the guest pack, for someone
with no place on the roster enrolling under a sponsor-agreed exception.

`wording.audience_for` chooses between them on a single fact — the preference
that authorised the enrolment. That was sufficient for as long as the two
audiences and the two preferences lined up one-to-one, which they did, but only
because of who EnrolDirect happened to admit. It was never a rule and it is not
enforced anywhere.

The consequence today is not an error. It is a document. A rostered applicant
admitted under Guest access falls through to the guest pack, and that pack
tells them we hold no member record for them and encloses an identity
confirmation form for them to complete and return. Both statements are false
about a person the sponsor has listed on its own roster, and the request is one
plan administration then has to withdraw by telephone. Nothing fails, nothing
is logged, and every individual pack reads as correct.

`GET /api/audit/selection-inputs` already reports this: it compares what a pack
claims about the recipient against what the roster says, and today's seeded
feed returns a contradiction count of 1.

Add the third audience and select it. A rostered applicant admitted under Guest
access receives a pack that acknowledges their place on the roster, does not
claim we hold no record of them, and does not ask them to prove who they are.
Their access is still guest access and the pack must not imply coverage they do
not hold.

Out of scope: no change to who is admitted, and no change to the two existing
packs. Admission is EnrolDirect's decision and the existing packs are correct
for the recipients they were written for.

Acceptance criteria:

AC1 — a rostered applicant admitted as a guest gets the new pack.
Given an enrolment record with `onRoster` true and an authorising preference of
Guest access, when its pack is built, then the pack's audience is the new
rostered-guest audience, its prose does not claim MapleSure holds no member
record, and its enclosures do not include the identity confirmation form.

AC2 — the two existing audiences are untouched.
Given any record that is either admitted under Member access, or admitted under
Guest access while not on the roster, when its pack is built, then its
audience, its five prose fields and its enclosure list are exactly what they
are today. This story adds a case; it does not re-word the existing ones.

AC3 — the determination is built from these rules.

- A new audience constant `AUDIENCE_ROSTERED_GUEST = "ROSTERED_GUEST_PACK"` in
  `repos/documenthub/wording.py`, added to `ALL_AUDIENCES` after the two
  existing entries.
- A `WORDING` entry for it, carrying all seven fields the other two carry. Its
  `assumesNoMemberRecord` is `False`, because the sponsor lists this person and
  a pack claiming otherwise is the defect being fixed. Its `relationship`
  paragraph states that the recipient is listed on the contract roster and that
  this enrolment gives them access as a guest without existing coverage. Its
  `nextSteps` must not ask the recipient to confirm their identity.
- `wording.audience_for` returns the new audience when the authorising
  preference is Guest access **and** `record.onRoster` is true, and is
  otherwise unchanged. It stays the single place in the service where an
  audience is chosen — no call site may test `onRoster` for itself.
- An `ENCLOSURES` entry in `repos/documenthub/enclosures.py` for the new
  audience: the benefit summary, the claims guide, the privacy notice, and
  `ROSTER_WELCOME`. It must not include `IDENTITY_FORM`.
- `packs.build_pack` and `packs.audience_breakdown` need no new branching. They
  already resolve the audience through `wording.audience_for` and already
  report every audience in `ALL_AUDIENCES`, including one with a count of zero.
- The two existing audiences are unaffected. Member packs and non-rostered
  guest packs keep working exactly as before — same audience, same prose, same
  enclosures — and `tests/test_regression_documenthub.py` passes without
  changes to it.

Not to be changed by this story:

- `repos/documenthub/feed.py`. It is the integration contract with EnrolDirect,
  which owns every field in it. DocumentHub must read `onRoster` and must never
  derive, default or correct it — a service that infers its own input can
  disagree with the system of record about the same person and still produce a
  confident document. The seeded records are the evidence this story is real
  and must keep their present values.
- `repos/documenthub/main.py`. The endpoints and their payload field names are
  consumed by the operator console and by the regression suite. The audit
  endpoint in particular must keep computing `contradictsRoster` from
  `Wording.assumesNoMemberRecord` rather than from which audience was selected
  — it is the independent check on the selection rule, and a check rewritten in
  terms of the rule it checks is not one.
- `repos/documenthub/static/index.html`. The console must keep rendering
  without edits.
- `tests/test_regression_documenthub.py`, which is the independent check that
  this story broke nothing and must pass unchanged before and after.
