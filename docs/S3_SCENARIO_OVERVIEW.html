MapleSure Insurance · Enhancement Delivery · Scenario Overview

Extending online enrolment to prospect members

What the business asked for, and what AI is expected to do about it — before we open the tooling.

1 · The application, and the change being requested

EnrolDirect is MapleSure's online enrolment channel — where someone on a group contract enrols in their benefit plan themselves, without calling anyone. Who is allowed to use it is decided by two access preferences the sponsor agrees at inception.

Today
MembersCan enrol online — on the roster, holding an active benefit
GuestsCan enrol online — off the roster, under a sponsor-agreed exception
ProspectsRefused — on the roster, no active benefit yet. Neither preference was written for them.
→
After this change
MembersUnchanged
GuestsUnchanged
ProspectsCan enrol online — classified and admitted under the guest access preference

The business request is one sentence: decide whether a prospect member is eligible for online enrolment, and return that decision through the channel's existing eligibility check. It arrives as a user story with acceptance criteria, on the delivery team's board.

2 · What AI is expected to do with it

Step 1
Understand the request

Reads the user story and the source code together, and asks the business a question when the story leaves something unsaid — rather than guessing and writing the wrong thing.

Human decidesAnswers the clarifying questions. Nothing proceeds until they do.
Step 2
Assess the impact

Names the components that have to change, sizes the effort, and identifies which other application teams this change also needs work from.

Human decidesAccepts the assessment and routes the work to an engineer.
Step 3
Write the code

Cuts a feature branch and drafts the change file by file, each one with its reason. Nothing is written to the repository until it is approved.

Human decidesPeer-reviews the diff, asks the AI why it made a change, requests amendments, applies or rejects.
Step 4
Prove it works

Designs the test scenarios first, then writes and runs the tests — and separately re-runs the pre-existing suite it is not allowed to touch, to show nothing else broke.

Human decidesQA reviews and edits the test plan, and approves it before any test code exists.
Step 5
Hand it over

Commits only once the tests actually pass, then produces the release notes, the deployment and rollback plan, and a record of what was — and was not — proven.

Human decidesApproves the release and closes the ticket.
The point of the demo

Every step above is AI-assisted and every step has a human gate. What follows is the same five steps, run for real against a working application — you will see the request arrive on the board, the code change proposed and reviewed, the tests pass, and the prospect who was refused a moment ago get through.

MapleSure Insurance is a fictional insurer; all data is synthetic. Scenario: US-2026-045 · EnrolDirect · prospect access at the online enrolment gate.
