01 · The Problem

Why this scenario exists

L1/L2 support teams lose time in three predictable places: chasing requestors for details a vague ticket never included, re-triaging and re-routing by hand, and hunting for a prior fix by memory or ad-hoc search. None of that is a knowledge problem — it's a drafting problem. S1 automates the draft at each of those three points and leaves a support engineer as the one who reviews, edits, and sends.

AI drafts everything; a support engineer reviews, edits if needed, and sends — except two scoped, clearly-labeled exceptions: the quality gate's own requestor info-request email, and self-service password-reset/ID-card requests. Nothing else in this flow is auto-sent or auto-closed. — demo/presenter_notes/s1_triage.md

That line is the design constraint everything else in this document sits under. It's not a caveat bolted on after the fact — see § Non-negotiable for how it's enforced in code, not just the demo script.

04 · What We Built, and Why

Capability by capability

1.0Data-quality gatequality_gate.py
What
Scores an incoming incident 0–1 on whether it's investigable as-is. If not, drafts a mail back to the requestor asking for the missing specifics — auto-sent, no engineer review.
Why
This is the differentiator, not just the ask — it stops an engineer from opening a ticket that's going to bounce straight back for more information. Calibrating the boundary took real live-model testing (see § Verification) — a single pass proved nothing near the line.
Self-service auto-resolveself_service.py
What
Password-reset and digital-ID-card requests matched on category=Access, priority=P4, and keyword text auto-resolve with a fixed template — no LLM call at all.
Why
The textbook "safe to fully automate" ITSM case: fully reversible, touches only the requestor's own account, no judgment call. Scoped narrow on purpose — "locked out" can just as easily mean a fraud report, so category+priority gates the keyword match.
1.1Two-level triage — rules, then AIrules_engine.py + triage.py
What
A plain-text, per-application rule file fires first. Its suggestion is handed to the model as context; the model makes the final call and must explain any override.
Why
An LLM doesn't know NightlyBatch's heap-growth pattern is a known recurring issue — the client's own team does. Rules encode that as an editable text file the client can change live, with an agreement gate that pauses for a human on deviation or a no-rule verdict.
1.2Assignment-group routingrouting.py
What
Maps the incident's application to its owning support group and cites how many prior resolved incidents from the same app/category were routed there.
Why
Deliberately deterministic, no LLM call — this is the highest-traffic beat in any rehearsal. It has to be instant and identical every time; a lookup table plus a scan is the right tool, not a model call.
1.3Similar-incident retrievalsimilar_incidents.py
What
TF-IDF cosine similarity over application, category, description, and known-error/problem linkage — ranked and returned with resolution notes attached, floor 0.05.
Why
Also deterministic by design — "seen 4 times before, here's what fixed it" has to be a fact you can trust, not a model paraphrase of one. Retrieval earns that trust; generation doesn't need to be involved until the next step.
1.4RAG resolution draftresolution.py + kb_retrieval.py
What
Grounds a drafted resolution — steps, work note, requestor email — in KB chunks and similar-incident notes actually retrieved, not general model knowledge.
Why
The highest-stakes output in S1: it's the one closest to reaching a requestor. Grounding it in retrieved, real prior resolutions keeps the draft anchored to what actually worked; the prompt itself forbids the model from claiming anything was sent or applied.
1.5Chatbot modechatbot.py
What
Free-text Q&A over the same incident and KB corpus, alongside the structured auto-draft flow.
Why
Not every question an engineer has fits the six-beat pipeline shape. Built cheaply because the retrieval helpers already existed for 1.3/1.4 — exposing them as open Q&A cost little extra.
Triage Funnel & live queue statusfunnel.py + queue_status.py
What
Forecasts, for the whole population and with zero LLM calls, how many incidents would even need a human to figure out what they are — then a live view overrides the forecast once a ticket is actually opened.
Why
The opening screen of the demo. Calling the real quality gate per incident just to render a dashboard would mean dozens of live calls on every page load — the rule engine and similarity score already answer the forecast question for free.

06 · Request Flow

One incident, eight beats

What app.py's Auto-draft mode runs, in order, for the engineer's selected incident.

1
Self-service check
match_self_service() checks category, priority, and keyword text before anything else runs.
No LLM
2
Quality gate
Incident text → complete(), cached per incident number → investigable verdict, score, and a drafted requestor mail if it isn't.
LLM call
3
Rule-first, AI-final triage
rules_engine.apply_rules() checks the app's rule file with no LLM involved, then its output is handed to the model as context for the final category/priority call.
Rules + LLM call
4
Agreement gate
Agree auto-proceeds. Deviation or AI-only (no rule fired) pauses the pipeline for the engineer to arbitrate.
Human action, conditional
5
Assignment-group routing
App→group lookup plus a precedent count scanned from incidents.csv — fully deterministic.
No LLM
6
Similar-incident retrieval
TF-IDF cosine similarity ranks prior incidents, resolution notes attached.
No LLM
7
RAG resolution draft
Relevant KB chunks selected by TF-IDF, combined with the similar-incident notes, and handed to the model for a grounded draft.
LLM call
8
Review & approve
Engineer clicks "Mark as reviewed & approved" — a session-only flag plus a timeline event. No API call, no ticket update, no email sent.
Human action

If any step's LLM call fails, app.py catches it once around the whole block, shows a warning, and leaves the already-rendered incident header intact — Streamlit reruns the full script on every interaction, so an unhandled exception would blank the page instead.

07 · Non-negotiable

Human-in-the-loop

AI suggestion — verify with your specialist before applying.

This exact string (common/constants.py:AI_SUGGESTION_LABEL) precedes every AI output rendered in app.py. It isn't a UI convention layered on top — quality_gate.render() and resolution.render() both hard-code language that the draft is for engineer review, and the resolution prompt itself instructs the model to never state that anything was sent or applied. The flow's contract ends at resolved + ticket updated; closure is explicitly the client's side, not ours.

Two named, narrowly-scoped exceptions exist and are labeled distinctly so neither generalizes into "the AI acts unsupervised": the quality gate's own info-request mail (AUTO_SENT_LABEL — a request for information, never a resolution or ticket action) and the self-service confirmation (AUTO_RESOLVED_LABEL — fully reversible, account-scoped, zero AI judgment involved).

10 · Risks

What could go wrong, and the mitigation

RiskMitigation
Quality gate is not fully deterministic near its calibration boundaryDocumented live on 2026-07-16 (datagen/SEEDS.md): the same template returned different verdicts across 3 live calls. Fix was to loosen the prompt's evidence bar, not just add examples — re-verify with 5x-trial checks, never a single pass, after any prompt change.
Shared .cache/llm wipes every scenario's cache on resetAcceptable while S1 is the heaviest cache consumer; revisit with a scenario-scoped cache_key prefix once S2/S3/S5/S6 all cache as heavily.
Rule file edited incorrectly live in front of the clientrules_engine._parse_rule_file raises immediately on a malformed line — rehearse the edit beforehand so the failure mode is known, not discovered live.
Client asks for misroute-scanning (US-1.6) liveIt's PLUS-priority and unbuilt, roadmap-only — say so, don't improvise it on stage.
Batch mode can't pause for arbitrationBy design — a headless run has no UI to pause into. A live deviation/AI-only case is flagged in its result note for follow-up instead of gated.
