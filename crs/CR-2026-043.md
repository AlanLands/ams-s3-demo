CR-2026-043: Benefit Claim Deductible Handling

Requested by: MapleSure Claims Operations
Application: ClaimsPortal (FastAPI claims intake + contract lookup services)
Priority: P3 - small enhancement

Description:
The claims intake service (claims_service) validates each submitted benefit
claim against the group contract it fetches live from the contract lookup
service (policy_service): the contract must exist, be ACTIVE, and the claim
amount must not exceed the contract's annual maximum. Contracts carry no
deductible today, so claims below what the plan member would owe out of pocket
are accepted and routed to adjudicators anyway, wasting handling time — and
accepted claims record no payable amount for downstream settlement.

Add a per-contract deductible and apply it during claim intake:

- Each contract record gains a deductible amount, returned by the contract
  lookup API and consumed by the claims service alongside status and annual
  maximum.
- A claim whose amount is at or below the contract's deductible is rejected as
  below-deductible.
- An accepted claim records a payable amount: the claim amount minus the
  contract's deductible (never negative). Rejected claims record a payable
  amount of zero.

Seed data: the in-memory demo contracts gain these deductibles — MS-1001: 500,
MS-1002: 1000, MS-1003: 500, MS-1004: 100 (same currency as their annual
maximums).

Acceptance criteria:

- Claim decision logic is centralized in a new `claim_rules` module
  (`apps/claimsportal/claims_service/claim_rules.py`). Its public API is a
  fixed contract the generated test suite depends on by these exact names:
  - `decide(policy_status: str, annual_maximum: float, deductible: float,
    amount: float) -> str` returns exactly one of:
    `"REJECTED_POLICY_"` + the status (for any non-ACTIVE contract status),
    `"REJECTED_OVER_LIMIT"`, `"REJECTED_BELOW_DEDUCTIBLE"`, or `"ACCEPTED"`.
  - `payable(amount: float, deductible: float) -> float` returns the claim
    amount minus the deductible, floored at zero.
- Decision precedence, checked in this order: non-ACTIVE contract status first,
  then amount over the annual maximum, then amount at or below the
  deductible; otherwise ACCEPTED.
- GET /api/policies and GET /api/policies/{policyNumber} on policy_service
  include each contract's deductible.
- POST /api/claims responses (and the stored claim record) include a
  payableAmount field — the claim_rules.payable amount for accepted claims,
  zero for rejected claims.
- The claims service's decision for a claim must come from
  claim_rules.decide, not inline logic in main.py.
- Existing flows keep working: contract list/detail, the claims contract
  directory passthrough, claim submission, and claim list. Both team consoles
  must keep rendering without changes to their HTML.
