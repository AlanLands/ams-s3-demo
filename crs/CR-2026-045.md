CR-2026-045: Prospect Member Eligibility Check For Online Enrolment

Requested by: MapleSure Group Retirement Product
Application: EnrolDirect (online enrolment channel)
Priority: P3 - enhancement

Business objective:
Determine whether a prospect member is eligible for online enrolment, and
return that determination through the channel's existing eligibility check so
the systems downstream of it can act on a real answer instead of a refusal.

Target member type:
A member who is on the plan sponsor's contract roster, has not enrolled in any
plan, and holds no active benefit. EnrolDirect already has a name for this
population — PROSPECT — and already distinguishes it from the two the access
preferences were written for: members, who hold coverage to change, and
guests, who have none and no place on the roster.

The determination is consumed by:

- self-registration on the member portal,
- access to the online enrolment channel itself,
- creation and use of the member directory record.

Description:
The eligibility check exists. `POST /api/eligibility/check` runs three gates —
the contract is active, the applicant's category resolves to an access
preference, the sponsor enables that preference — and returns a decision that
carries its reasons rather than a bare boolean.

What is missing is an answer for a prospect. Neither access preference was
written for someone on the roster holding no active benefit, so a prospect
resolves to no preference at all and is refused at the second gate. That
refusal is not a decision anyone took. It is the narrower option in force by
default, and every day it stands is a day the sponsor's rostered people are
turned away by an omission rather than by a rule.

The impact analysis that sized this is complete. It asked which of the two
existing preferences a prospect should be checked against, evaluated both
options against the whole book, and recommended Guest as the narrower grant.
The analysis is done; the gate has not been changed.

Assign the recommended option and enforce it. A prospect is checked against the
Guest preference: the member-facing screens have nothing to show someone
holding no coverage, widening access later is easier to justify than
withdrawing it, and it matches how the rest of the estate already treats people
with no active benefit under a contract.

The classification decides two things, not one, and both are in scope. At the
gate it decides how many prospects are admitted. Past the gate it decides how
much of the benefit catalogue those admitted can reach, because member-only
plans attach to existing coverage. The two effects run in opposite directions
and the change is not complete if only the first is made.

Out of scope: no new access preference, and no per-contract variation. Both
would change what PolicyCore stores and what every consumer of these
preferences reads. The two preferences stay exactly as they are; what changes
is which of them a prospect resolves to.

Acceptance criteria:

AC1 — a prospect member is eligible.
Given a member on the contract roster holding no active benefit, when they pass
every gate, then the check reports them eligible and the decision names the
preference that admitted them.

AC2 — a prospect member is not eligible.
Given the same member, when they fail any one gate, then the check reports them
not eligible and the decision carries the reason. Every existing refusal code
keeps its current meaning: a prospect refused because their sponsor did not
enable the resolved preference is refused at the gate (`NO_CHANNEL_ACCESS`),
not at the catalogue.

AC3 — the determination is built from these rules.

- The prospect policy is a single named value in
  `repos/enroldirect/applicants.py`, set to treat prospects as guests, sitting
  alongside the two options it selects between. It is module-level
  configuration, not a per-call parameter: the gate enforces one policy, and a
  caller that could pass its own would be choosing its own access rules.
- `eligibility.preference_for_category` resolves a prospect through that policy
  to one of the two existing preferences. Members and guests keep resolving
  exactly as they do now, and the function stays the single place a category
  becomes a preference.
- A new `eligibility.effective_category` returns the category an applicant is
  treated as for the rest of the journey — a prospect's is the policy's value,
  a member's and a guest's is their own. Resolved once and reused, not derived
  again at each call site.
- The three gates keep their current order: contract active, then category
  resolves to a preference, then the sponsor enables that preference. A lapsed
  contract must still deny a prospect regardless of the policy.
- The access decision records which policy resolved the applicant's preference,
  populated for prospects and null for everyone else. `authorisingPreference`
  keeps meaning the preference that actually opened the gate, so a prospect
  admitted as a guest is recorded as such — that is what DocumentHub words the
  confirmation pack from and what NightlyBatch reconciles on.
- The enrolment path applies the same effective category when testing a
  member-only plan, and reuses the access gate rather than reimplementing it.
  The enrolment record carries the effective category and the policy applied,
  on the same terms as the access decision.
- The benefit catalogue endpoint reports the applicant's effective category and
  filters availability on it, so the catalogue and the gate cannot disagree
  about the same person.

Not to be changed by this CR:

- `impact.py` and the `/api/analysis/*` endpoints. The analysis is the input to
  this change, not part of it. It has to keep sizing *both* options after one
  has been adopted — a comparison that reported only the option in force could
  not answer "what is this costing us", which is the question that reopens the
  decision. Its JSON field names are consumed by the console and stay as they
  are.
- `benefits.plans_open_to`, which already takes an effective category and needs
  no change to receive a resolved one.
- `static/index.html`. The console must keep rendering without edits.
- `tests/test_regression_enroldirect.py`, which is the independent check that
  this CR broke nothing and must pass unchanged before and after.
