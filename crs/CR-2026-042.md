CR-2026-042: Amendment Priority Field

Requested by: MapleSure Support Operations
Application: PolicyCore (group benefits plan administration portal)
Priority: P4 - small enhancement

Description:
Plan sponsors can request a contract amendment (a change to an in-force
group contract, e.g. changing a plan tier, adding or removing a
dependant, or updating the sponsor's details on file) through a 5-field
request form: amendment type, requested change, effective date, contact
phone, and contact email. Support engineers currently have no way to tell
which submitted amendment requests are time-sensitive versus routine, so
urgent requests sit in the same unsorted queue as routine ones.

Add a 6th field to the amendment request form: a priority level the plan
sponsor (or the support engineer on their behalf) selects at submission time,
either "Standard" or "Urgent". Requests default to "Standard" so existing
submission behavior is unaffected unless a requester deliberately flags
urgency.

Acceptance criteria:

- The amendment request form has a "Priority" field with exactly two
  choices, "Standard" and "Urgent", defaulting to "Standard".
- Submitting a request without changing the priority still succeeds exactly
  as before this CR.
- The stored amendment record persists the selected priority.
- Existing contract list, contract detail, plan-member roster, claim
  submission, and claim list flows are unaffected.
