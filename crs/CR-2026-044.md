CR-2026-044: Flag Urgent Endorsement Requests

Requested by: MapleSure Support Operations
Application: PolicyCore (group benefits plan administration portal)
Priority: P4 - small enhancement

Description:
This request came in from the support floor rather than through the usual
change-request desk, so it names no target system beyond the application.

When a plan sponsor asks us to change an in-force group contract — a
coverage level, a dependant, the sponsor's details on file — the request
form gives us no way to say whether it is time-sensitive. Everything lands
in one unsorted queue, so a contract change a sponsor needs before a payroll
cutoff sits behind routine housekeeping until someone reads the whole queue.

Support Ops want the requester to be able to mark a request as urgent at the
point of submission, with routine requests continuing to behave exactly as
they do today.

Acceptance criteria:

- A requester can mark an endorsement request as urgent when submitting it,
  choosing between exactly two levels, routine and urgent.
- Requests are routine unless the requester deliberately marks them urgent,
  so existing submission behavior is unchanged.
- The chosen level is persisted with the stored endorsement record.
- Existing contract list, contract detail, plan-member roster, claim
  submission, and claim list flows are unaffected.
