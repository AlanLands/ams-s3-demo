CR-2026-041: Plan-Tier Upgrade Option

Requested by: MapleSure Product Team
Application: PolicyCore (group benefits plan administration portal)
Priority: P4 - small enhancement

Description:
Plan sponsors currently cannot change a group contract's plan tier once
the contract is in force - any change requires a manual back-office process.
Add a tier-upgrade option to the plan administration portal so a plan
sponsor (or the support engineer on their behalf) can upgrade an active
contract's plan tier, e.g. Standard -> Premium -> {{TIER_NAME}}, directly
from the contract detail view, for an additional monthly contribution that is
calculated automatically.

Acceptance criteria:

- A group contract has a plan tier (Standard, Premium, or {{TIER_NAME}}).
- From the contract detail view, the tier can be upgraded to a higher tier
  (no downgrades in this CR).
- The contract's monthly contribution is recalculated automatically to reflect
  the new tier and persisted.
- Existing contract/claims flows (list contracts, view the plan-member roster,
  submit a claim) are unaffected.

Known downstream considerations for other teams (do not size, risk-assess, or
scope this section into PolicyCore's own CR above - it is each named team's
own separate backlog item, entirely their own effort and timeline, not
additional work or risk on this CR):

- BillingGateway's invoice generation today only ever recalculates a group
  contribution at renewal, on its own cycle. A tier upgrade changes the
  contribution mid-cycle, so BillingGateway needs a new mid-cycle handling path
  (e.g. a pro-rated adjustment or a makeup line on the plan sponsor's next
  invoice) - simply reading the new contribution number is not enough on its
  own.
- ClaimsPortal's claims-eligibility check reads a contract's plan tier to
  determine what a plan member's claim is covered for. A claim already filed
  before an upgrade must keep being evaluated against the tier that was active
  at the time it was filed, not a later upgrade - ClaimsPortal needs to confirm
  it captures tier-at-filing-time rather than re-reading the contract's current
  tier.
