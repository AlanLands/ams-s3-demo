CR-2026-041: Coverage-Upgrade Option

Requested by: MapleSure Product Team
Application: PolicyCore (policy/claims portal)
Priority: P4 - small enhancement

Description:
Policyholders currently cannot change their coverage tier once a policy is
active - any change requires a manual back-office process. Add a
coverage-upgrade option to the policy portal so a policyholder (or the
support engineer on their behalf) can upgrade an active policy's coverage
tier, e.g. Standard -> Premium -> {{TIER_NAME}}, directly from the policy
detail view, for an additional premium amount that is calculated
automatically.

Acceptance criteria:
- A policy has a coverage tier (Standard, Premium, or {{TIER_NAME}}).
- From the policy detail view, the tier can be upgraded to a higher tier
  (no downgrades in this CR).
- The policy's premium is recalculated automatically to reflect the new tier
  and persisted.
- Existing policies/claims flows (list policies, submit a claim) are
  unaffected.

Known downstream considerations for other teams (do not size, risk-assess, or
scope this section into PolicyCore's own CR above - it is each named team's
own separate backlog item, entirely their own effort and timeline, not
additional work or risk on this CR):
- BillingGateway's invoice generation today only ever recalculates a premium
  at renewal, on its own cycle. A tier upgrade changes the premium
  mid-cycle, so BillingGateway needs a new mid-cycle handling path (e.g. a
  pro-rated adjustment or a makeup line on the next invoice) - simply
  reading the new premium number is not enough on its own.
- ClaimsPortal's claims-eligibility check reads a policy's coverage tier to
  determine what a claim is covered for. A claim already filed before an
  upgrade must keep being evaluated against the tier that was active at the
  time it was filed, not a later upgrade - ClaimsPortal needs to confirm it
  captures tier-at-filing-time rather than re-reading the policy's current
  tier.
