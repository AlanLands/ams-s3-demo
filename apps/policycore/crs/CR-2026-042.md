CR-2026-042: Endorsement Priority Field

Requested by: MapleSure Support Operations
Application: PolicyCore (policy/claims portal)
Priority: P4 - small enhancement

Description:
Policyholders can request a policy endorsement (a change to an active policy,
e.g. updating coverage details or personal information on file) through a
5-field request form: endorsement type, requested change, effective date,
contact phone, and contact email. Support engineers currently have no way to
tell which submitted endorsement requests are time-sensitive versus routine,
so urgent requests sit in the same unsorted queue as routine ones.

Add a 6th field to the endorsement request form: a priority level the
policyholder (or the support engineer on their behalf) selects at submission
time, either "Standard" or "Urgent". Requests default to "Standard" so
existing submission behavior is unaffected unless a requester deliberately
flags urgency.

Acceptance criteria:
- The endorsement request form has a "Priority" field with exactly two
  choices, "Standard" and "Urgent", defaulting to "Standard".
- Submitting a request without changing the priority still succeeds exactly
  as before this CR.
- The stored endorsement record persists the selected priority.
- Existing policy list, policy detail, claim submission, and claim list flows
  are unaffected.
