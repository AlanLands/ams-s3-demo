CR-2026-043: Claims Deductible Handling

Requested by: MapleSure Claims Operations
Application: ClaimsPortal (FastAPI claims intake + policy lookup services)
Priority: P3 - small enhancement

Description:
The claims intake service (claims_service) validates each submitted claim
against the policy record it fetches live from the policy lookup service
(policy_service): the policy must exist, be ACTIVE, and the claim amount must
not exceed the policy's coverage limit. Policies carry no deductible today, so
claims below what the policyholder would owe out of pocket are accepted and
routed to adjusters anyway, wasting handling time — and accepted claims record
no payable amount for downstream settlement.

Add a per-policy deductible and apply it during claim intake:
- Each policy record gains a deductible amount, returned by the policy lookup
  API and consumed by the claims service alongside status and coverage limit.
- A claim whose amount is at or below the policy's deductible is rejected as
  below-deductible.
- An accepted claim records a payable amount: the claim amount minus the
  policy's deductible (never negative). Rejected claims record a payable
  amount of zero.

Seed data: the in-memory demo policies gain these deductibles — MS-1001: 500,
MS-1002: 1000, MS-1003: 500, MS-1004: 100 (same currency as their coverage
limits).

Acceptance criteria:
- Claim decision logic is centralized in a new `claim_rules` module
  (`apps/claimsportal/claims_service/claim_rules.py`). Its public API is a
  fixed contract the generated test suite depends on by these exact names:
  - `decide(policy_status: str, coverage_limit: float, deductible: float,
    amount: float) -> str` returns exactly one of:
    "REJECTED_POLICY_" + the status (for any non-ACTIVE policy status),
    "REJECTED_OVER_LIMIT", "REJECTED_BELOW_DEDUCTIBLE", or "ACCEPTED".
  - `payable(amount: float, deductible: float) -> float` returns the claim
    amount minus the deductible, floored at zero.
- Decision precedence, checked in this order: non-ACTIVE policy status first,
  then amount over the coverage limit, then amount at or below the
  deductible; otherwise ACCEPTED.
- GET /api/policies and GET /api/policies/{policyNumber} on policy_service
  include each policy's deductible.
- POST /api/claims responses (and the stored claim record) include a
  payableAmount field — the claim_rules.payable amount for accepted claims,
  zero for rejected claims.
- The claims service's decision for a claim must come from
  claim_rules.decide, not inline logic in main.py.
- Existing flows keep working: policy list/detail, the claims policy
  directory passthrough, claim submission, and claim list. Both team consoles
  must keep rendering without changes to their HTML.
